        Markov Chain Language Model

        Predicts the next word from the previous N−1 words using empirical transition probabilities from the training corpus.

        How it works: The model looks at your last word (or last 2 for trigram) and counts how often each next word followed it in the training corpus. Wider bar = more likely next word.
