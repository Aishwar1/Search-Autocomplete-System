          Markov Chain Language Model

          A Markov chain predicts the next word from the previous N−1 words. P(wₙ | wₙ₋₁) for bigrams. Yahoo's early search used n-gram models exactly like this. Each edge weight is the empirical transition probability from the training corpus.

            Gradient Descent on Loss Surface

            Gradient descent minimizes the loss by moving in the direction of the negative gradient: θ ← θ − η·∇L(θ). The 3D surface below is the Rosenbrock function — a classic optimization challenge. Watch the trajectory converge to the global minimum.

            📐 Update Rule
            θₜ₊₁ = θₜ − η · ∇L(θₜ)
            Vanilla SGD update
            L(w₁,w₂) = (1−w₁)² + 2(w₂−w₁²)²
            Rosenbrock banana function

            Gradient-Boosted Decision Trees (GBDT)

            GBDT builds trees sequentially, each fitting the residual of the previous: Fₘ(x) = Fₘ₋₁(x) + η·hₘ(x). Used by Bing and Yandex for query ranking. Below you see Tree #1 of the ensemble and feature importances for your query.
